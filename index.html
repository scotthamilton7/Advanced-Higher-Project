Why is a good CV important?

Your CV is the first impression potential employers will have of you. Your CV demonstrates professionalism and highlights your key skills and experience. It is vital to make a positive first impression which clearly outlines why you are the correct person for the job and a good CV can significantly increase your chances of being considered for the job opportunities.
